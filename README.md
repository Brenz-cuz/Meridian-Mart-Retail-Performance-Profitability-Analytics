# Meridian-Mart-Retail-Performance-Profitability-Analytics

**Tableau retail analytics Project | Executive reporting, discount strategy and operational performance**

I developed a two-page Tableau dashboard to bring Meridian Mart's commercial performance into one analytical view. The project examines **sales, profitability, regional performance, discounting, product losses, customer segments, and fulfilment efficiency**, with recommendations aimed at improving margin quality rather than revenue alone.

## Dashboard previews

### 1. Executive Performance

![Meridian Mart Executive Performance dashboard](Meridian%20Mart%20Overview.png)

This dashboard brings together sales, profit, profit margin, distinct orders, regional performance, monthly and annual trends, product categories, city-level profitability and customer segments.

### 2. Profitability & Operations

![Meridian Mart Profitability and Operations dashboard](Meridian%20Mart%20Dashboard.png)

This page focuses on loss-making subcategories, discount-profit patterns, shipping volumes, delivery speeds, regional discount patterns and top customers.
